Unheart Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Unheart — Bulk Unlike for X does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data.

  • All processing happens locally in your browser on x.com.
  • Settings (panel position, first-run confirmation, your X username) and run progress are saved only on your device using Chrome's local extension storage.
  • Unheart does not read your passwords, cookies or login tokens, and does not contact any server.
  • Uninstalling Unheart removes all of its stored settings.

Unheart is not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp.
