How it works

  1. Install Unheart and click its icon — it opens your Likes on X.
  2. Press Unlike all (or try 10 first) and confirm once.
  3. Keep the tab visible while it runs — put its window side by side with your other work.

Questions

Can I undo it?

No. Removing a like can't be undone, which is why Unheart asks you to confirm once and lets you try 10 first.

Why isn't it instant?

X limits how fast likes can be removed. Unheart runs at about one per second at best and slows down when X asks, so your run finishes instead of getting blocked.

Some old likes are still there.

X sometimes stops loading very old likes on the Likes page. Reload the page and run Unheart again to catch more of them.

Does Unheart collect my data?

No. Everything happens in your browser. See the privacy policy.
